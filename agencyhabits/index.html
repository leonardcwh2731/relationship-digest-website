Your inbox, handled with care.

Connecting your email is a big ask. Here’s exactly what we do with it.

We only get read-only access.

We request only gmail.readonly, so we can’t send, delete or change anything in your inbox.

We do not save any emails.

Email bodies and attachments are deleted after processing. We keep only names, companies, roles, last contact dates and short thread summaries. When our team reviews your picks, we pull what we need live, then discard it.

Independently tested every year.

Our application passes ESOF AppSec, an annual security assessment by TAC Security, a Google-approved assessor.

Switch it off anytime.

Remove our access from your Google Account permissions page in one click. Workspace admins can also do it in the Admin console. It takes effect immediately.
