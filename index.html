The Alternatives

Claude, Codex, or any other AI tools.

They are a great place to start and save cost at first. But as you dive deeper, you’d soon face with a couple of problems.

01

Lack nuances.

They read your messages and vaguely label each contact as hot, warm, or cold. They don’t know your industry, your work, or how creative agencies win work. So the quietly valuable relationships never get surfaced.

The cost Miss out relationships that actually matters.

02

Broken data layer.

They are used to amplify the power of your relationships, not be the source of truth. So leaning on them means you’d be scaling the enrichment and signals monitoring system faster, but in a wrong direction.

The cost Scale the wrong things fast.

03

Heavy maintenance.

After building out the system, you still have to do the real work: fine-tuning prompts, fixing bugs, maintaining data subscriptions, optimizing signals, and more. Which often takes a team to maintain.

The cost Still takes a lot of time.

Who This Is For

This is not for every creative agency.

We work best with a specific kind of team. Here’s how to know if that’s you.

This is for you if…

  • Your per-project deal size is $25K and above, or your best clients come back — so one reconnected relationship pays for itself many times over.
  • You have one or more dedicated sellers focusing on business development on your team.
  • Your agency depends on relationships and referrals for new business and opportunities.
  • You want a partner who combines relationship instinct with technical depth — so you reach more opportunities and close more deals.
  • You treat Gmail as your main hub — it’s where your real relationships and history actually live.

This is not for you if…

  • Your work is small, high-volume, and one-off — clients rarely return, so reconnecting with one relationship doesn’t move the needle.
  • Nobody owns business development — sales is entirely founder-led with no dedicated seller in place.
  • Your new business doesn’t really run on relationships or referrals — it comes from ads, inbound, or one-off transactions.
  • You want software you run yourself, not a partner working the relationships alongside you.
  • Your relationships don’t run through Gmail, so there’s little history for us to scan and resurface.
Your Privacy

Your inbox, handled with care.

Connecting your inbox isn’t a small thing. Here’s how we manage the risk.

01

We only request for read-only access.

Gmail’s API is divided into five core scopes. Each one grants a different level of reach into your mailbox:

  • Read-only gmail.readonly View messages, attachments, and metadata. Nothing can be changed. What we request
  • Modify gmail.modify Read, create, and modify threads, messages, and labels.
  • Compose gmail.compose Create, update, and delete drafts — and send them.
  • Send gmail.send Send email on your behalf.
  • Full access mail.google.com Complete control, including permanent deletion.
02

We extract what we need and destroy the rest.

Storing your emails wouldn’t make the product better — it would slow every query, raise our compute costs, and create risk we have no reason to carry.
