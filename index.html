Who This Is For

This is not for every creative agency.

We work best with a specific kind of team. Here’s how to know if that’s you.

This is for you if…

  • Your per-project deal size is $25K and above, or your best clients come back — so one reconnected relationship pays for itself many times over.
  • You have one or more dedicated sellers focusing on business development on your team.
  • Your agency depends on relationships and referrals for new business and opportunities.
  • You work with multiple stakeholders in a project, from the decision maker to the junior lead.
  • You treat Gmail as your main hub — it’s where your real relationships and history actually live.

This is not for you if…

  • Your work is small, high-volume, and one-off — clients rarely return, so reconnecting with one relationship doesn’t move the needle.
  • Nobody owns business development — sales is entirely founder-led with no dedicated seller in place.
  • Your new business doesn’t really run on relationships or referrals — it comes from ads, inbound, or one-off transactions.
  • Your projects are solo or single-contact, so there’s no web of stakeholders to reconnect with.
  • Your relationships don’t run through Gmail, so there’s little history for us to scan and resurface.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Does inbox-sifting involve multiple people, or just one person?

It can be either. Some teams have only one person connected, while others may need multiple team members involved. We think it is more valuable for the team members themselves to use it, since they are the ones with the more active client relationships — though we understand you may want to start with yourself first. Scanning team members’ inboxes is also useful because if a team member leaves, the company still keeps the relationship asset.

How often is the digest sent, and how many contacts are in each digest?

The digest is sent once every weekday and includes three contacts per digest. There is also a weekly recap, a monthly recap, and a quarterly recap.

What happens if a contact is wrong or the LinkedIn data is inaccurate?

We try to verify that the LinkedIn profile matches the real person you contacted. If the profile is wrong, you can tell us it is the wrong person. If we cannot find a LinkedIn URL, there is not much we can do about it, but we will monitor it on our side.

How far back should inbox history go to get meaningful results?

Ten years is typically the sweet spot. That is usually enough history to uncover a large and useful pool of past relationships without going so far back that the data becomes too noisy or difficult to clean.

Why does the solution send three people per day?

The logic is that outreach compounds: one good introduction can turn into replies, follow-ups, and meetings, which then create more work and more opportunity. Sending too many recommendations each day can reduce follow-through and make the process feel like noise. Hence, we believe that three contacts per day is the sweet spot.

How is my data protected when I give inbox access?

We have enterprise security certifications through an application security framework, and the company is certified by a Google-partnered security provider. We also do not save full email bodies. Instead, during onboarding we scrape the emails for relevant information, summarize it, and save snippets and summaries rather than the raw email content.

Can I get the extracted contact data if I want it?

Yes. We’re open to sharing them should you not plan to continue after the first 6 months.
