The Problem

Your relationships go cold between projects... so does your pipeline.

It’s not that they don’t want to work with you again. It’s that you disappeared. You were heads-down on the next project. You were in delivery mode. You were busy putting out fires. And the relationships you’ve built quietly went cold.

Who This Is For

Relationship Digest is not for everyone.

This is for you if…

  • Your agency is in the $5M–$10M ARR range and you’re looking to grow with existing relationships.
  • You have at least one dedicated business developer helping you build relationships and generate new business.
  • You’ve been running your digital or creative agency long enough to have thousands of dormant relationships with real revenue potential.
  • You want more… not because you’re struggling, but because you know your network is underutilized.
  • You treat consistent follow-up as a professional responsibility, not a sales tactic.

This is not for you if…

  • You’re looking for a self-service tool to configure and leave running without engagement.
  • You don’t have anyone owning business development… sales is still entirely founder-led, with no dedicated business developer.
  • You’re comfortable with your current revenue and not actively looking to grow.
  • You want a cold outreach machine… we don’t find new people, we resurface people who already know you.
  • You have a scarcity mindset about your network and your market.

Your Privacy

Connecting your inbox isn’t a small thing. Here’s how we manage your risk...
and some hard truths.

1 / 4

We extract what we need and destroy the rest.

Storing your emails doesn’t make our product better, in fact, it makes it worst. Because they take up memory, slow down every database query, and make our cron jobs run longer... which means higher compute costs on every single run. And storing them creates risks we have no reason to carry.

That’s why we do not save any sensitive email content.

We extract the contact signals we need and destroy everything else immediately... and the access we have is read-only which means we can never send, delete, or modify anything in your inbox.

Your inbox has already been exposed. Probably more than once.

Your inbox has actually been exposed...if you’ve ever connected a CRM, personal relationship tool, or any email-related app. Their teams have access.

That’s just how it works.

So the risk isn’t connecting your inbox to our tool. It’s leaving your dormant contacts sitting there, unreactivated.

You’re always one click from revoking everything.

If anything ever feels wrong, or you notice something unexpected, or you simply change your mind... you can revoke access instantly from your Google account settings.

No need to email us. No contract tied to data connection.

Just note that if you’re still an active user, we won’t be able to track your outreach and update your contacts’ information anymore.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How do you access my email and is my data private?

We connect via Google OAuth with read-only access. We extract contact signals and destroy everything else immediately. Storing your emails doesn’t make our product better, in fact, it makes it slow and expensive. Also, we can never send, delete, or modify anything in your inbox. We don’t have a dashboard where anyone reads your threads, and we don’t sell or share your data. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account settings.

How long does setup take?

It happens across two 30-minute calls. First, a discovery call to see if we’re a fit. If we are, we send over the agreements and invoices. Once those are signed and the upfront payment is in, we book a second 30-minute call to connect your Gmail and Calendar and capture your goals. Your first digest lands within 48 hours.

What if a suggested contact’s email bounces?

Every contact goes through email enrichment and verification before it appears in your digest. We check against LinkedIn profiles and company domains. If we can’t verify a high-confidence email, the contact won’t appear. If one slips through, let us know and we’ll troubleshoot it.

Can I customize what contacts are suggested?

Yes. During your discovery call, we talk through your quarterly goals, ideal company, and ideal client profile. That context goes directly into the system before your first digest. Over time, the system also learns from your outreach... which angles get replies, which contact types convert... and adjusts accordingly.

What happens on the monthly call?

Every month we review your snapshot together... digests sent, contacts reached, replies, meetings booked, and which signals are converting. We use that session to tune your account... adjusting signal weights, refining your ICP context, updating draft patterns. It’s not a status update. It’s a calibration that makes the next month sharper than the last.

Is this a self-service tool?

No. Relationship Digest is a managed service. We handle the onboarding, the network scan, the signal monitoring, and the monthly calibration. You receive a weekday digest and act on it. No dashboard. No logins. No manual data entry. The system runs. You reach out.

How is this different from outbound agencies?

They find new people. We resurface people who already know your work. Outbound agencies generate cold meetings with clients who’ve never seen your portfolio. We generate warm conversations with clients who’ve already worked with you. They’re actually complementary... outbound for new, us for existing.
