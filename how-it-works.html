How it works

Every brief starts as a conversation. Usually with someone you’ve known for years.

This is the work our team does behind the scenes so those conversations happen at the right time. You spend about twenty minutes a day. We do the rest.

Before you write
Who matters, and what was said last time.
When you write
A note in your voice, that lands in the inbox.
After you write
Who’s paying attention, and who to follow up.
Every week
A report, and every relationship in one place.

What we run for you

Six parts, in the order you’ll feel them.

FeatureWhat it doesWhat you get
01Relationship strategy Maps your full inbox and calendar history, finds the real person behind every email address (even after a job change), and ranks each relationship by recent signals, past interactions, their network, and how well their company fits your ideal client. A clear view of who matters most, and why, before anyone reaches out.
02Relationship context Connects to your meeting notetaker, so every email and calendar thread carries what was said, who attended and who spoke. Every reconnection starts from what was actually said.
03Relationship signals Shows who visits your website: people who don’t know you yet, and people already in your network, including those who read your note but didn’t reply. Follow-ups go to people who are already paying attention.
04Inbox health Monitors your sending reputation and inbox placement, especially when cold email goes out from your main address. Your notes land in the inbox, not spam, and your name stays trusted with the people who matter most.
05Weekly strategy report Covers strategy at two levels: day to day (who we pick, what context we use, how each message is written) and the why behind it (why this person, why not others, why now). Plus progress with dormant and new relationships, inbox health, and security and privacy. Peace of mind, and a clear view of what we’re doing and why.
06Client dashboard Shows everything in the report, live: who to contact, who to follow up with and drafted follow-ups, who you’ve reached out to, conversations started, current and all-time numbers, former and current client accounts, inbox health, and security and privacy. One place to see every relationship in motion.

Who you work with

Account manager

Relationship strategist

Sets the high-level strategy: why this person, why not others, why now.

Relationship engineer

Keeps your relationship strategy, context and signals connected and accurate.

Copywriter

Writes each note in your voice, from the context we have.

Email specialist

Looks after your inbox health and sending reputation.

Your part

About twenty minutes a day. The rest is ours.

What we do

Pick who to talk to each week and explain why now. Pull the context from past emails, meetings and notes. Draft each note in your voice. Watch who’s paying attention and suggest follow-ups. Keep your inbox healthy. Report every week, and review your key accounts with you each quarter.

What you do

Read the suggestions, edit anything that doesn’t sound like you, and send from your own inbox. Take the calls that come back. Tell us what you heard, so the next suggestion is sharper.
