Case study · A VP Marketing at a major science museum

Inside a private RFP three months before the project went public.

The Client hadn’t emailed this contact in 8.5 years. Eleven days after one note, the Client was invited into a private, NDA-only RFP for a major exhibition. The project wasn’t made public for another three months. It is now a $67K campaign.

The relationship

In 2017 the contact was VP Brand Management at an agency and hired the Client for a product video. Their last email was in October 2017. In March 2026 she became VP Marketing and Communications at a major science museum.

What VeraOps flagged

March 30, 2026

She topped the Client’s weekly list from VeraOps, last contacted about 102 months earlier.

Why now
“Job change to a senior marketing role at a major cultural institution, prime moment to reconnect as she’s establishing new production partnerships.”
Suggested message
“[Name], Just saw you landed at [the museum]. That’s a really cool move… We worked together back at [agency] on the [product] video. That was a fun project, a lot of moving parts and you kept it all together. Would love to catch up and hear what you’re building over there.”

How the conversation unfolded

  1. Apr 3The Client sends the suggested note almost word for word. She replies 23 minutes later: “It would be great to catch up… let’s connect towards mid-late April.”
  2. Apr 14The brief: the museum’s Director of Marketing invites the Client to a private RFP for “an early-stage project… featuring a widely-known IP”. NDA required.
  3. Apr 17RFP shared, “tailored based on our understanding of your agency’s specialties”. Budget: $100K for ideation, shoot and post.
  4. Apr 23Catch-up call with the contact.
  5. May 11Proposal submitted.
  6. May 29Pitch to the museum team.
  7. Jun 25Decision moved: no third-party shoot this summer, paid media from Q4.
  8. Jul 7First public signal: she announces she is leading the exhibition.
  9. Sep 21–24Creative concepts reopen; in-person meeting.
  10. Oct 14$58K estimate for the exhibition campaign.
  11. Oct 23Photography and retouching added. Revised to $67K.
  12. Nov 9Kick-off call.

All dates 2026.

What it shows

No feed would have surfaced this project in April. It was under NDA, and the public announcement came three months later. The Client was on the RFP list because they reconnected within days of her new job, not after the news broke.
