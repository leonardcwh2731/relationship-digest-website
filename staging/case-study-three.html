Case study · A Partner at a newly launched creative agency

First to reconnect. First production partner.

A contact who had been quiet for 21 months moved to a new agency. The Client reached out six days after VeraOps flagged the move. Within a week of their catch-up call, he had mentioned a prospect. Eight weeks later that prospect became a $50K brief, and the Client became the new agency’s first production partner.

The relationship

The Client met the contact in June 2024, when he ran creative operations at a video production marketplace. Their last exchange, in October 2024, was about a cancelled project. He said he wanted to work together on future opportunities. In May 2026 he became a Partner at a newly launched creative agency.

What VeraOps flagged

July 14, 2026

VeraOps’ contact refresh sent the Client a job-change alert.

The alert
“[Name], Partner, [agency]. Last contacted: about 21 months ago.”

How the conversation unfolded

  1. Jul 20The Client writes, 6 days after the alert: “Saw you’ve started a new studio… Congrats!”
  2. Jul 29He replies and agrees to a call.
  3. Jul 31Catch-up call.
  4. Aug 6He introduces his co-founder and a prospect: a NYC fintech that had just raised, looking for thought-leadership video. “They’ll be serious about production quality, so I therefore turn to you guys!”
  5. Aug 7The Client sends a lightbox of relevant work for him to pitch with.
  6. Aug 26VeraOps suggests a follow-up; the Client checks in the same day.
  7. Sep 30The brief: “Upcoming Project in NYC”, for that same fintech.
  8. Oct 7Scope set at $50K.
  9. Oct 9Verbal commitment from the end client.
  10. Oct 16Kick-off call.
  11. Oct 20He writes: “thanks for being our inaugural partner!”
  12. Oct 26–27Statement of work sent for signature; production day booked.
  13. Nov 4–5Pre-production call, then the Client meets the end client’s team in person.

All dates 2026.

What it shows

His move was public, and anyone could have seen it. The brief was not: it began as a prospect he mentioned a week after the catch-up call, eight weeks before it became a project. Reaching him first made the Client his new agency’s “inaugural partner”.
