What you’ll hear

Everyone sees the headline. You hear what’s behind it.

Signal feeds tell every studio the same thing at the same time: a job change, a layoff, a funding round. A conversation with someone who already trusts you tells you what it means for the next six to twelve months.

Illustrative examples, not client data
What the feed showed

A 12% layoff at a consumer brand.

Every studio reads it the same way: budgets are frozen. Nobody pitches.

What the conversation revealed

It was a pivot, not a freeze.

The cuts were in retail. The brand is moving spend to direct-to-consumer and needs a new library of product films before the spring launch.

What it means for you: a brief in about four months, and a chance to shape it now.

What the feed showed

A new CMO at a mid-market software company.

Every studio sends a congratulations note in the same week, then waits for the RFP.

What the conversation revealed

She’s rebuilding the agency roster.

She plans to cut from six agencies to two in her first ninety days, and wants partners she has worked with before.

What it means for you: the roster is decided before any RFP is written.

What the feed showed

A merger announced between two regional banks.

It looks like a rebrand is coming. Every branding studio starts preparing a pitch.

What the conversation revealed

Budgets are frozen until the deal closes.

Nothing moves for two quarters. The rebrand is real, but it will be scoped by whoever is still talking to the team when the freeze lifts.

What it means for you: stay close through the quiet months, and don’t pitch into a freeze.

The difference

A signal tells you something happened. A conversation tells you what happens next, and whether you’re part of it.
