Data Handling Policy

Last modified: November 13, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to BVS I LLC d/b/a VeraOps (“VeraOps”).

VeraOps (“us”, “we”, or “our”) operates the VeraOps Relationship Digest service (hereinafter referred to as “Relationship Digest” or the “Service”).

This Data Handling Policy (the “Policy”) describes how we access, use, protect, retain and delete the data you connect to Relationship Digest, including data obtained from your Google account. It applies solely to Relationship Digest and to no other VeraOps product or service.

By connecting your Google account to Relationship Digest, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

Relationship to Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

This Policy supplements, and forms part of, our Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service (the “Terms”). Terms used but not defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in the Privacy Policy or the Terms.

In the event of any conflict between this Policy and the Privacy Policy or the Terms concerning data processed through Relationship Digest, this Policy shall prevail.

Definitions

Client means the individual or organization that has subscribed to Relationship Digest and connected a Google account to the Service.

Google User Data means any data obtained from a Client’s Google account through Google APIs, including Gmail and Google Calendar data.

Refresh Token means the long-lived credential issued by Google when a Client authorizes Relationship Digest, which permits the Service to request Access Tokens on the Client’s behalf.

Access Token means the short-lived credential issued by Google in exchange for a Refresh Token, which permits a single period of read-only access to a Client’s Google account.

Decryption Key means the secret used to decrypt a Client’s stored Refresh Token.

Service Providers means the third parties that process data on our behalf to operate Relationship Digest, as listed in the Service Providers section below.

Information We Access

Relationship Digest accesses a Client’s Google account on a strictly read-only basis. The Service cannot send, delete, modify, label or move any email or calendar event.

Access is granted by the Client through Google’s own authorization screen. We do not receive, request or store the Client’s Google password.

Information We Retain

We retain only the information necessary to prepare and deliver the Service and to measure its results. This includes:

  • The Client’s Refresh Token, in encrypted form only, as described under Security of Google Account Credentials
  • Contact records derived from the Client’s correspondence, including name, email address, employer, role, and relationship signals such as the date of last contact and message counts
  • Suggestions and draft messages contained in each digest, and any edits the Client makes to them
  • Outcome records, including whether a suggested contact replied or scheduled a meeting
  • Account and onboarding information
  • A log of each occasion on which the Client’s mailbox was accessed

We do not retain:

  • The Client’s Google password
  • Access Tokens, which are generated when required, used, and discarded
  • Payment card details, which are processed by our payment processor in accordance with the Privacy Policy
  • Email threads or any other sensitive information within the Client’s mailbox

Security of Google Account Credentials

The Refresh Token is the credential that permits Relationship Digest to access a Client’s Google account. We encrypt each Refresh Token before it is stored, and we hold the Decryption Key in a separate, restricted environment that is independent of our database.

Accordingly, a copy of our database alone would contain only encrypted Refresh Tokens, without the means to decrypt them.

Authorization

When a Client connects a Google account:

  1. The Client signs in through Google and approves read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar.
  2. Google issues a Refresh Token to Relationship Digest.
  3. The Refresh Token is encrypted immediately and stored in our database in encrypted form only.
  4. The Decryption Key is not stored in our database, our source code or our code repository. It is held as an encrypted secret within our cloud hosting environment.

Each Access

Each time Relationship Digest accesses a Client’s mailbox or calendar:

  1. Our cloud hosting environment releases the Decryption Key to our application server at runtime. Only our production application, and authenticated VeraOps administrators, can access the Decryption Key.
  2. The application server decrypts the Client’s Refresh Token for that request only.
  3. The application server presents the Refresh Token to Google over an encrypted connection and obtains a new Access Token. Access Tokens issued by Google expire after approximately one hour.
  4. The Access Token is used for that operation only. It is held in memory and is never written to disk or to our database.
  5. On completion of the operation, the decrypted Refresh Token and the Access Token are discarded.

Because a new Access Token is obtained for every access, no standing credential exists that could be used against a Client’s account independently of these controls.

Encryption and Security Safeguards

All data transmitted between the Client, Relationship Digest, Google and our Service Providers is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections.

All data held in our database is encrypted at rest by our database provider. Refresh Tokens are subject to an additional, independent layer of encryption, as described under Security of Google Account Credentials.

Application secrets, including API keys and the Decryption Key, are never committed to source code. They are held only as encrypted secrets within our cloud hosting environment.

We maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect the data we process. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

Relationship Digest’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular:

  • We use Google User Data solely to provide and improve the user-facing features of Relationship Digest.
  • We do not use Google User Data for advertising, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not sell Google User Data.
  • We do not transfer Google User Data to third parties except as necessary to provide the Service through the Service Providers listed below, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.
  • We do not use Google User Data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Our personnel access Google User Data only as described under Personnel Access.

Use of Artificial Intelligence

Relationship Digest uses an artificial intelligence model to analyze relevant correspondence and to prepare the suggestions and draft messages contained in each digest. We provide the model only with the content necessary for the specific task, such as recent correspondence with a particular contact.

We access the model through our AI provider’s commercial API. Under the applicable commercial terms, the provider does not use data submitted through the API to train its models.

We do not use Client data to train any artificial intelligence model, whether our own or a third party’s. Where a Client edits a draft message, we may use those edits to reflect the Client’s writing style in that Client’s future digests. Such edits are not used for, or shared with, any other Client.

Personnel Access

Relationship Digest is a managed service. A limited number of authorized VeraOps personnel access Client data in order to deliver it, and only to the extent necessary for the following purposes:

  • To review and quality-check digest suggestions before delivery to the Client
  • To respond to a support request made by the Client
  • To investigate security incidents or abuse
  • To comply with applicable law

Personnel access a Client’s mailbox only through read-only tools. Each such access is recorded in the Client’s activity log.

We do not permit any person outside VeraOps and our Service Providers to access Client data.

Revocation of Access, Retention and Deletion

Revocation by the Client

A Client may revoke Relationship Digest’s access to its Google account at any time, without notice to us, through the third-party connections settings of its Google account. On revocation, the stored Refresh Token ceases to function and the Service can no longer access the Client’s mailbox or calendar.

Retention

We retain Client data for as long as the Client’s subscription remains active and as necessary to provide the Service.

Deletion on Termination

On termination or non-renewal of a Client’s subscription, we will:

  1. Remove the connection to the Client’s Google account, and delete and revoke the Client’s Refresh Token
  2. Delete the Client’s data from our active systems, including contact records, digests, draft messages and outcome records

Data held in backups is deleted in the ordinary course as those backups expire, which may take up to one month.

Contact lists and other data compiled by the Service are not provided to the Client on termination. A former Client who later resumes the Service will be onboarded as a new Client.

Legal Retention

We may retain limited records, such as invoices, where required to comply with applicable law.

Security Incidents

If we become aware of unauthorized access to Client data, we will notify the affected Client without undue delay. Our notice will describe the nature of the incident, the data affected, and the measures we are taking in response.

Where a Refresh Token may have been compromised, we will revoke it and request that the Client reconnect its Google account.

Your Choices

A Client may request at any time that we correct or delete specific data we hold about it. Requests should identify the data to be changed or removed and may be sent to relationships@veraops.com. We will act on such requests within thirty (30) days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will notify Clients of any material change by email or by a prominent notice on our website prior to the change becoming effective, and we will update the date at the top of this Policy.
