Relationship Digest gives you standard signals. Relationship Revenue layers in custom signals built specifically for your agency, your ICP, and your growth goals. These signals evolve over time, based on what's working.

Social Signals

LinkedIn posts, mentions, job changes, promotions, new ventures… anything happening on social media, we capture it and push it into the system.

News Signals

Fundraising, product launches, M&A, executive changes, hiring, partnerships, regulatory shifts, and more. 60+ signal categories monitored continuously.

Outcome: See how each relationship evolves… where it came from, how you've engaged, and what signals matter.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How is this different from Relationship Digest?

Relationship Digest is a daily email that surfaces who you should reach out to and why. Relationship Revenue takes that intelligence and builds a complete system around it: CRM integration, done-for-you activation sequences, meeting transcription, data enrichment, and strategic advisory. Digest tells you who to talk to. Revenue makes sure those conversations turn into pipeline.

Do I need a CRM?

Not necessarily. If you already have a CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.), we'll integrate directly with it. If you don't, we can help you set one up as part of Phase 04. The system is designed to make your CRM actually useful, not to add another tool you won't use.

What about my existing meeting recorder?

If you already use a meeting recorder (Otter, Fathom, Fireflies, etc.), we can work with your existing tool. If you don't have one, meeting intelligence is included in Phase 03. We'll handle the transcription, summarization, and action item extraction so everything feeds back into your CRM and relationship system.

How long before I see results?

This is a long-term system built to scale your agency. Early wins come within the first month as activation sequences start generating conversations. But the real value compounds over time. As you grow, you won't have to worry about a broken system, waste time checking scattered tools, or scramble to piece things together. Everything is already connected, enriched, and running. It's not a quick fix — it's taking your relationship operations to the next level so scaling feels effortless.

What if I have other tools besides inbox, calendar, meeting recorder, and CRM?

We're more than happy to help you connect them all together. The goal isn't about which specific tools we integrate — it's about making your existing relationship operations streamlined and interconnected. If you have tools that hold relationship data, we'll find a way to bring them into the system.

Is my inbox data safe?

Yes. We use read-only Gmail API access — we can never send, delete, or modify your emails. We use Google OAuth 2.0 with granular scopes, and your data is processed in encrypted environments. We extract relationship metadata (who, when, how often), not email content. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account settings.

What agencies is this built for?

Relationship Revenue is designed for project-based agencies where revenue depends on relationships: video production companies, creative agencies, digital agencies, PR firms, consulting practices, and similar service businesses. If your business grows through repeat clients, referrals, and network relationships rather than paid advertising, this system is built for you.
